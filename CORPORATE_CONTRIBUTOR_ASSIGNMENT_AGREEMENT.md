# Corporate contributor assignment agreement

This agreement is between the organisation in the signature block (the **Corporation**) and MAGRATHEAN UK LTD (the **Company**).

## 1. Authorised contributors

Corporation will maintain and provide a schedule of employees/contractors authorised to submit Contributions. Removal does not revoke rights already transferred.

## 2. Authority

Corporation represents that it owns or controls sufficient rights in each Contribution and has obtained employee, contractor and moral-right consents.

## 3. Assignment

Corporation assigns absolutely to Company all present copyright, database, design and analogous rights in each accepted Contribution, including rights of action, for the full term and worldwide.

Corporation presently assigns future copyright and otherwise holds future rights on trust pending formal transfer.

## 4. Licence back

Company grants Corporation a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use Corporation's own Contributions, excluding Company confidential information, trade marks and endorsement rights.

## 5. Patent licence

Corporation grants Company and Project recipients a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free patent licence under claims controlled by Corporation necessarily infringed by a Contribution as submitted.

## 6. Disclosures

Corporation will identify third-party material, standards obligations, generated code, known patents, employer/client restrictions and licence duties.

## 7. Records and warranty

Corporation will keep records proving authority and warrants that Contributions do not knowingly contain unauthorised confidential information, personal data, credentials, malicious code or material it lacks authority to assign.

## 8. Law

English law applies. The courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction, subject to mandatory law.
