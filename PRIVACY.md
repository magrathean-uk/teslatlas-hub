# Privacy

Teslatlas Hub is local-first software. It stores and processes the account,
token, vehicle, telemetry, and location data needed for the features you enable
on your own machine or service host.

The project does not operate a telemetry, analytics, or account-collection
service. Requests needed for vehicle access necessarily go to Tesla-operated or
other user-configured third-party services. Their terms and privacy practices
apply.

You control deployment, backups, retention, access, and deletion of locally
stored data. Protect database files, logs, exports, and tokens. Do not publish
them in issues or pull requests.
