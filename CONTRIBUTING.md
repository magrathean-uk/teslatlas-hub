# Contributing

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that you have the right to submit it
and license it under the repository licence: GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later.

Keep changes minimal, testable, and free of secrets, personal data, copied
third-party source, or undisclosed licence obligations. Do not add build output
or local credentials.

Open an issue before large changes. Sign each commit with `Signed-off-by:` to
certify the [Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/).
This confirms that the work is yours, is permissibly derived, or is submitted
with authority, and that it may be distributed under this project's licence.
