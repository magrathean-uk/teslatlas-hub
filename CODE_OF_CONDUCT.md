# Code of conduct

Teslatlas Hub contributors and maintainers must keep project interaction safe,
professional, and focused on verifiable technical work.

## Expected conduct

- communicate respectfully and address the work, not the person;
- welcome good-faith questions and clearly explain safety or legal boundaries;
- use reproducible evidence and correct errors openly;
- protect credentials, personal data, vehicle location, and private reports;
- preserve attribution and disclose consulted, adapted, generated, or copied
  material;
- stop testing when another person's system, account, data, or physical safety
  could be affected;
- accept maintainer decisions on scope, release integrity, security embargoes,
  and legal compliance.

## Unacceptable conduct

- harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, or personal attacks;
- publishing private information or secret-bearing telemetry;
- unsafe vehicle commands, unauthorized testing, phishing, credential abuse,
  denial of service, or destructive payloads;
- plagiarism, origin misrepresentation, licence removal, or false endorsement;
- retaliation against a good-faith reporter or contributor.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `contact@magrathean.uk` with subject
`CODE OF CONDUCT`. Include the relevant repository location, date, people
involved, and evidence. Do not include unrelated personal data.

MAGRATHEAN UK LTD may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject
contributions, restrict participation, or refer credible safety or legal risks
to the appropriate authority. Reports are handled as privately as reasonably
possible, but confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where disclosure is legally
required or necessary to protect people or systems.

This policy governs project spaces controlled by MAGRATHEAN UK LTD. It does not
authorize testing or conduct on third-party services.
