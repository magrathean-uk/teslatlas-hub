# Trade marks and branding

## Magrathean marks

MAGRATHEAN UK LTD claims rights, subject to applicable law and earlier rights, in the names and branding it uses for **Magrathean**, **Teslatlas** and **Teslatlas Hub**.

The software copyright licence does not grant a trade mark licence.

## Permitted factual use

Without separate permission, a person may:

- preserve legal and attribution notices;
- identify unmodified software truthfully;
- state truthful compatibility or origin;
- use a name nominatively or descriptively where law permits.

## Permission required

Written permission is required to:

- use a Teslatlas or Magrathean logo as the primary identity of a fork;
- imply official status, certification, partnership or endorsement;
- market a modified build as an official Magrathean release;
- use confusingly similar branding for a distribution; or
- register an account, domain or product name likely to imply Company control.

A materially modified distribution should use a distinct primary brand and state that it is independently distributed.

## Third-party marks

Tesla and its vehicle, product and charging names are marks of their owners. TeslaMate and its visual identity are associated with the TeslaMate project. Apple, PostgreSQL, Cloudflare, OpenStreetMap and other marks belong to their owners.

References are factual compatibility or attribution statements only. No affiliation is claimed.

## Product name decision

The product name remains **Teslatlas**. This policy provides risk controls and consistent use; it does not propose another name.
