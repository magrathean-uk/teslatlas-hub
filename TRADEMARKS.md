# Trade marks and branding

## Magrathean marks

Magrathean UK Ltd claims rights, subject to applicable law and third-party rights, in the names and branding it uses for **Magrathean** and **Teslatlas**. A copyright licence to software does not grant a trade mark licence.

No representation is made in this file that a mark is registered in every territory or that use of a mark is free from conflict with an earlier right.

## Permitted descriptive use

Without separate permission, a person may:

- preserve required copyright, licence and attribution notices;
- identify unmodified software truthfully;
- state truthful compatibility or origin information;
- refer to Magrathean or Teslatlas where applicable law permits nominative or descriptive use.

## Uses requiring written permission

Written permission is required to:

- use a Magrathean or Teslatlas logo as the primary identity of a fork, product or service;
- state or imply endorsement, certification, partnership or official status;
- market a modified build as an official Magrathean release;
- use a confusingly similar name or get-up for a competing distribution;
- register a domain, account, company or product name that is likely to imply Magrathean control.

Forks should adopt their own name and visual identity and describe origin in a secondary factual notice.

## Third-party marks

Tesla, Tesla vehicle model names, associated logos and other Tesla branding are marks of their respective owners. TeslaMate and its visual identity are associated with the TeslaMate project and its rightsholders. Apple, macOS, Linux distribution names and other third-party marks belong to their owners.

Use of a third-party name is for truthful identification or compatibility only. No affiliation, sponsorship or endorsement is claimed.

## Material unresolved risk

The project name **Teslatlas** includes the word element “Tesla”. Disclaimers do not eliminate trade mark, passing-off, platform or contract risk. In particular, an agreement governing an official API may impose naming rules broader than trade mark law.

Before a stable commercial launch, Magrathean must complete UK and EU clearance, consider United States clearance, review platform rules and decide whether to rename. No contributor or distributor should rely on this policy as a representation that the name is cleared.

## Reporting

Send a substantiated branding complaint to `contact@magrathean.uk` with subject `LEGAL NOTICE`. Identify the mark, territory, registration or right relied upon, exact use complained of and requested action.
