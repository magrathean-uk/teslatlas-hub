# Founder contribution policy

The founder's public attribution name is **Gyorgy Bolyki**.

The founder does not execute a contributor agreement with himself or a new
Company title instrument for each commit.

Before an official release, founder-created Product intellectual property must
be covered by an executed confirmatory deed or another applicable Company title
record. This repository does not assert that a private instrument was executed
or remains sufficient; the authorised release signer verifies that fact from
current Company records.

For new public Hub commits, the founder must still:

- use DCO sign-off;
- disclose third-party and generated material;
- preserve upstream notices;
- avoid moving proprietary app source into Hub without approval;
- follow branch/review/security rules.

The absence of a separate founder CLA is intentional. Whether the private deed
and Company records are complete is a factual release attestation made by an
authorised maintainer; this public policy does not prove execution or title.

Historical pre-beta commits without DCO trailers are disclosed in
`HISTORICAL_CONTRIBUTIONS.md` and must not be silently rewritten.
