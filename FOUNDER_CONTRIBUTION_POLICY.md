# Founder contribution policy

The founder's public attribution name is **Gyorgy Bolyki**.

The founder does not execute a contributor agreement with himself or duplicate the Company's private confirmatory deed for each commit.

Founder-created Product intellectual property is covered by the confirmatory deed and Company records.

For public Hub commits, the founder must still:

- use DCO sign-off;
- disclose third-party and generated material;
- preserve upstream notices;
- avoid moving proprietary app source into Hub without approval;
- follow branch/review/security rules.

The absence of a separate founder CLA is intentional and must not be treated as a missing contribution record once the deed is executed.
