# Contributor rights policy

Teslatlas Hub uses DCO sign-off plus a private copyright assignment for
non-trivial external contributions. This gives the project a clear public
submission trail and enough rights to maintain, enforce and, where rights permit,
license accepted project material separately.

## External contributions

Before merge, a non-trivial external contribution requires:

- DCO 1.1 sign-off on every commit;
- an executed individual or corporate contributor assignment;
- identity and authority verification;
- a provenance disclosure; and
- an entry in the private contributor register.

The canonical templates are
`docs/governance/individual-contributor-assignment-agreement.md` and
`docs/governance/corporate-contributor-assignment-agreement.md`. Executed
agreements, signatures, addresses and identity records stay in encrypted
Company-controlled storage, not GitHub.

## Company-covered work

No separate per-commit assignment is needed when signed employment, director,
contractor or founder records already place the contribution within Company
ownership. DCO sign-off still applies. The release signer verifies the relevant
private record before publication; public documentation is not a substitute for
that record.

## Trivial changes

A maintainer may accept a typo, formatting fix or similarly non-substantive
change under DCO alone when it introduces no protectable implementation and the
exception is recorded.

Opening a pull request does not itself sign or imply an assignment. A
contributor may stop future work, but rights already validly granted for accepted
contributions remain effective.
