# Contributor licence agreement policy

## Why a CLA is used

The project needs clear rights to distribute, enforce, maintain and, for Company-controlled contributions, potentially dual-license material across the public Hub and separate proprietary products.

## External contributions

A non-trivial external contribution requires:

- DCO 1.1 sign-off on each commit;
- an executed individual or corporate contributor assignment agreement;
- identity and authority verification;
- a provenance disclosure;
- entry in the private contributor register.

Canonical instruments: `docs/governance/individual-contributor-assignment-agreement.md` and `docs/governance/corporate-contributor-assignment-agreement.md`. Executed agreements are stored privately by Magrathean UK Ltd. GitHub contains templates and status only, never signatures, home addresses or identity documents.

## Company-owned contributions

No separate assignment is required where a contribution is already Company-
owned through employment, director duties, contractor assignment, or another
executed title instrument. DCO sign-off remains required. The founder does not
sign an assignment for each commit. Any applicable confirmatory deed or other
title record must be verified privately before release; this policy does not
assert that one exists.

## Trivial changes

A maintainer may accept a typo, formatting or similarly non-substantive change under DCO alone if no proprietary dual-licensing right is required, no protectable implementation is introduced and the exception is recorded privately.

## No retroactive assumption

A contributor is not treated as having signed merely by opening a pull request. Unexecuted templates create no assignment.

## Withdrawal

A contributor may stop making future contributions. Rights already validly granted for accepted contributions remain effective.
