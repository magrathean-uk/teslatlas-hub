# Founder contribution policy

**György Bolyki** is the original creator, architect and lead developer of
Teslatlas Hub.

He does not sign a contributor agreement with himself or execute a new Company
assignment for every commit. Company ownership of founder-created material is
governed by applicable law and the relevant signed Company records. Before an
official release, the authorised release signer verifies that those records
cover the released work; this public policy is not proof of the private record.

Founder contributions follow the same engineering controls as other project
work:

- DCO sign-off on new public commits;
- disclosure of third-party, adapted and generated material;
- preservation of upstream notices;
- explicit approval before moving proprietary Teslatlas source into Hub; and
- branch, review, security, provenance and release gates.

Historical pre-beta commits without DCO trailers are disclosed in
[Historical contribution record](historical-contributions.md) and are not
rewritten to create a certification that was not recorded at the time.
