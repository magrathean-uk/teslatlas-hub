# Authorship and stewardship

## Creator and technical leadership

**György Bolyki** created Teslatlas Hub and is its original architect and lead
developer. He leads technical direction across local data custody, provider
integration, telemetry collection, TeslaMate migration, authenticated sync,
security boundaries and release design.

This credit identifies project origin and leadership. It does not claim that
one person owns every contribution or third-party component.

## Publisher and project steward

**MAGRATHEAN UK LTD** publishes and maintains the official repository. The
Company controls official release status, accepts contributions, maintains
project policy and manages the Teslatlas names and visual identity subject to
applicable law and third-party rights. A release exists only when its complete
signed evidence set is explicitly published.

## Contributors and third parties

Accepted contributors remain credited through Git history, release notes and
applicable notices. Copyright ownership follows applicable law and signed
agreements; project credit does not replace that analysis.

TeslaMate, dependencies, upstream tools, data providers and other third-party
material retain their own authorship, copyright, licence and trade-mark
notices.
