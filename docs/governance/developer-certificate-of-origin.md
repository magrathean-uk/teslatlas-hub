# Developer Certificate of Origin

This project uses Developer Certificate of Origin version 1.1.

By signing off a commit, the contributor certifies that:

1. the contribution was created in whole or in part by the contributor and the contributor has the right to submit it under the indicated open-source licence; or
2. the contribution is based on previous work that, to the contributor's knowledge, is covered by an appropriate open-source licence and the contributor has the right to submit modifications under the same licence; or
3. the contribution was provided directly by another person who certified one of the above and the contributor has not modified it; and
4. the contributor understands the contribution and sign-off are public and may be retained indefinitely.

Add the sign-off using:

```sh
git commit -s
```

The sign-off must use a real identity or a legally accountable corporate identity approved by the maintainer. A pseudonym may be accepted only where the private CLA and contributor register establish the legal person behind it.

A DCO sign-off is not a substitute for the contributor assignment agreement where `docs/governance/cla-policy.md` requires one. See `docs/governance/developer-certificate-of-origin-1.1.md`.
