# Contributor assignment agreement — organisation

This agreement is between:

- the organisation that signs below (the **Organisation**); and
- MAGRATHEAN UK LTD, a company registered in England and Wales with company
  number 16955343 (**Magrathean**).

Magrathean keeps signed copies private and never publishes them.

## 1. Contributions

**Contribution** means any code, documentation, test, design or other material
that an Authorised Contributor submits to Magrathean for Teslatlas Hub on the
Organisation's behalf and that Magrathean accepts.

**Authorised Contributor** means an employee or contractor of the Organisation
listed in the schedule. The Organisation may update the schedule by written
notice. Removing a person does not reverse any assignment already made.

## 2. Assignment

2.1 The Organisation assigns to Magrathean, with full title guarantee, all
copyright, database rights, design rights and similar rights anywhere in the
world in each Contribution. The assignment is for the full term of those rights
and includes the right to sue for past infringement.

2.2 For Contributions made after signing, this agreement assigns the future
copyright and other future rights in them.

2.3 If any right does not pass to Magrathean under this agreement:

- the Organisation holds it on trust for Magrathean until it is assigned; and
- until then, the Organisation grants Magrathean an exclusive, irrevocable,
  worldwide, royalty-free licence of it, with the right to sublicense.

## 3. Licence back

Magrathean grants the Organisation a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive,
royalty-free licence to use, modify and distribute its own Contributions for
any purpose. The licence does not cover Magrathean's trade marks or
confidential information.

## 4. Licensing and credit

4.1 Magrathean may license the Contributions on any terms, including the GNU
Affero General Public License and commercial terms. Open-source licences
already granted to recipients stay in force.

4.2 Magrathean will credit the Organisation and its Authorised Contributors as
authors of the Contributions in the project's Git history and release notes.

## 5. Patents

The Organisation grants Magrathean, and every recipient of Teslatlas Hub, a
perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, irrevocable licence under any patent the
Organisation controls that a Contribution, as submitted, necessarily
infringes.

## 6. The Organisation's promises

The Organisation confirms that:

(a) it owns or controls the rights in each Contribution and is entitled to
assign them;

(b) it has obtained any consents and moral-rights waivers it needs from its
employees and contractors;

(c) each Contribution identifies, when submitted, any material its Authorised
Contributors did not create, with its source and licence; and

(d) no Contribution contains confidential information, credentials, personal
data or malicious code.

The Organisation will keep records that show its authority, and will provide
them to Magrathean on request.

## 7. No obligation

Magrathean need not accept, use or keep any Contribution. Apart from the
promises in clause 6, the Organisation provides its Contributions "as is".

## 8. Law

This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales. The courts of
England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
