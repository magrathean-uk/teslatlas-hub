# Open-source enforcement policy

MAGRATHEAN UK LTD prefers compliance and cure over punitive enforcement.

## Process

1. verify Company or contributor standing;
2. preserve the exact release and evidence;
3. identify covered material and licence clause;
4. distinguish copyright, trade mark and contract issues;
5. contact the distributor with a precise cure request;
6. honour GNU AGPL reinstatement/cure rules;
7. escalate only after legal review.

## Common cures

- publish exact Corresponding Source;
- restore notices;
- mark modifications;
- provide the network source offer;
- remove misleading official branding;
- correct source/binary mismatch.

## No misuse

Do not use AGPL to impose a competitor ban, proprietary-client ban or field restriction. Do not issue a DMCA notice for a trade mark-only complaint.

The Company may enforce Teslatlas trade marks separately.
