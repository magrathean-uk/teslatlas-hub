# Provenance

Teslatlas Hub is independently authored Rust software intended to interoperate
with Tesla account and vehicle data services and to support migration from
TeslaMate-compatible data.

Public TeslaMate behaviour, schema, and source material were consulted during
compatibility work. No TeslaMate source files or assets are distributed in this
repository. This is not a clean-room claim.

Teslatlas Hub is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or approved by
TeslaMate or Tesla, Inc. Compatibility may change when third-party services or
formats change.
