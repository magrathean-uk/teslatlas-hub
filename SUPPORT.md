# Support policy

Teslatlas Hub is community software supplied without a support commitment under the GNU AGPL.

## Community support

Public issues may be used for reproducible bugs, documentation defects and feature proposals. Do not submit secrets, personal data, legal demands or undisclosed vulnerabilities.

A response, fix, compatibility update or release date is not guaranteed.

## Commercial support

Any paid support, hosted service, warranty, service level or indemnity must be agreed in a separate written contract with Magrathean UK Ltd. Payment for another product does not create support rights for this repository.

## Supported environments

Only operating systems, architectures, database versions and installation methods listed in a tagged release are supported for that release. Planned Linux support is not a warranty that every distribution, init system, filesystem or packaging format will be supported.

## Forks and modified builds

Magrathean does not support modified or third-party builds unless it agrees otherwise in writing. A fork must not represent itself as an official supported release.
