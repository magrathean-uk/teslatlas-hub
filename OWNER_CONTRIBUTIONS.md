# Company owner, founder, director and employee contributions

Magrathean UK Ltd is the project steward and copyright holder of original Company-controlled material, subject to third-party and contributor rights.

A person contributing within Company duties must have one of:

- employment terms assigning or vesting relevant IP;
- a director/founder confirmatory and future-works assignment;
- a contractor assignment;
- another signed instrument approved by the Company.

That person does not sign a CLA with the Company for every commit. The relevant title instrument is recorded privately.

Every commit must still carry DCO sign-off. This preserves a consistent public submission trail and certifies that the contributor has authority to submit the work under GNU AGPL version 3 only.

Personal Git author names do not change Company ownership and do not determine public creator-credit spelling.
